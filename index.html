The Mooseheads faced off against Drummondville on Saturday night at the Scotiabank Centre in downtown Halifax. It was the first time the two teams met this season.

The Mooseheads were coming off a 6-2 victory over Rouyn-Noranda on Thursday evening, while the Voltigeurs had fallen 5-1 in Charlottetown the night before — their third game in three nights, and their seventh straight on the road to open the year.

Nathan Langlois opened it at 16:40 of the first on a feed from Malik L'Italien. Eliott Belzil tied it 5:38 into the second. Nobody scored again for 54 minutes.

Carson Mertz stopped 27 of 28 through overtime, then all five Drummondville shooters. Nathan Langlois, the fifth Halifax shooter, scored the only goal of the shootout.

Eddy Doyle took a five-minute boarding major and a game misconduct at 7:21 of the third. Halifax killed the whole thing. Drummondville managed three shots in the period.

The voice in the press box

This is Garreth MacDonald's tenth season behind the Halifax Mooseheads' mic. He joined the team in 2017–18. Broadcast credits include the 2023 IIHF World Juniors on TSN Radio and the 2025 World U17 Hockey Challenge on TSN. A former TV reporter with CTV in Halifax, he has provided play-by-play for the AUS, Maritime Junior A Hockey League, World Sledge Hockey Challenge, and the TELUS Cup.
